Support & FAQ

Everything you need to get started, customize controls, and play comfortably in Stratafall on Apple Vision Pro.

Quick Start

  1. Open Stratafall and select Play (or Resume Saved Game if you paused an earlier session).
  2. Place and size the 3D well on your floor or table. You can pinch-drag the handles or use the Bring to Me, Turn −15° / +15°, and size buttons directly on the panel.
  3. Double-tap the board (or tap Confirm) to lock placement, enter a local nickname, and select Start Game.
  4. Fill an entire horizontal layer to clear it cleanly and trigger crystalline fracture scoring.

Playing Without Hand Gestures

Every action in Stratafall is playable with standard gaze-and-tap or pointer controls—hand tracking is never required:

  • Tap the Controls button on the HUD ornament beside the board (or open Settings → Play) for full button parity: lateral movement, clockwise and counter-clockwise 3-axis rotations (Pitch, Yaw, Roll), Soft Drop, Hard Drop, Hold, Pause, and Bring Board to Me.
  • All interactive buttons include explicit capsule hit targets and hover highlights for Dwell Control and Switch Control compatibility.

Comfort & Accessibility Options

  • Seated Mode (Settings → Controls): Positions the well closer at tabletop height with compact gesture reach zones.
  • Reduce Motion: Automatically follows your visionOS Reduce Motion system setting—fracturing blocks gently dissolve in place without flying shards, banners cross-fade cleanly, and floating score indicators appear without travel.
  • Color-Blind-Safe Identity: Default Mineral Strata pigments separate every piece shape by luminance, and toggling Distinguish without color embosses distinct 3D surface vein patterns plus geometric HUD motifs.
  • Break Reminders & VoiceOver: Configure optional gentle break prompts in Settings → Controls, and enable VoiceOver for labeled HUD readouts and clear/level-up announcements.

Permissions & Troubleshooting

  • Re-enabling Room Sensing or Hand Tracking: If you declined spatial permissions on launch, open visionOS Settings → Apps → Stratafall, enable the toggle, then return to Stratafall and choose Retry (or close and reopen the board).
  • Lost Track of the Board: Tap Bring Board to Me on the HUD to recenter the well comfortably in front of your current viewpoint without resetting your score.
  • Pausing & Backgrounding: Pressing the Digital Crown or stepping out pauses both falling pieces and physics simulations automatically. When you return, your game waits paused until you choose Resume.
