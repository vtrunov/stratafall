Stack falling mineral polycubes directly in your room.

Stratafall brings volumetric block-stacking puzzles natively to visionOS. Anchor the three-dimensional lattice on your table or floor, sculpt complete horizontal layers with natural hand gestures or accessible button controls, and watch cleared strata fracture into physical mineral shards.
